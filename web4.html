SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL
Senior High School (SHS) is the last two years of the K to 12 curriculum and includes Grades 11 and 12.
In SHS, we equip our learners with the skills that will better prepare them for
the future whether employment, entrepreneurship, skills development (further tech-voc training), and higher education (college).

Admission Requirements
  • New students and transferees

    1. Original and photocopy of Report Card (Form 138)
    2. Recommendation Letter or Certificate of Good Moral Character signed by the Principal or Guidance Counselor from previous school
    3. Two (2) photocopies of NSO Birth Certificate
    4. Two (2) photocopies of Baptismal Certificate
    5. Two (2) pcs. long folders with fasteners
    6. Four (4) pcs. 1×1 ID picture
    7. Entrance Examination (free)

  • Foreign students

    1. Academic documents authenticated by the Philippine Embassy and/or Consulate official located in the student’s country of origin (with English translation, if written in foreign language)
    2. Student’s Personal History Statement
    3. Photocopy of student’s passport showing biopage, latest arrival and authorized stay in the Philippines
    4. Original and photocopy of Bureau of Quarantine-issued medical certificate
    5. Original and photocopy of birth certificate
    6. Three (3) pcs. 2×2 ID picture
    7. Three (3) pcs. 1×1 ID picture
    8. Two (2) pcs. long folders with fasteners
